About This Project

Project Overview:

This interactive map displays GIS Master's programs across the United States. Each program is categorized by its primary focus, Professional, Research, or Mixed, to help prospective students, educators, and researchers explore GIS graduate education across the U.S.

This visualization is one of the map products developed for my M.S. thesis titled “GIScience Education from Faculty Careers to Student Training: A Dual Perspective on Academic and Professional Development.”

Related publications in this series:

1. Geographic Information Science and Technology Master’s Education in the United States: Longitudinal Trends, Demographics, and a National Inventory of Active Programs

2. Evaluating GIS Professional Graduate Program Curricula in the United States

3. Where are GIScience faculty hired from? Analyzing faculty mobility and research themes through hiring networks

Data Sources:

• Program information manually collected from university websites

2026 update: Read the annual inventory change summary.

Features:

• Interactive filtering by state, program type, and format
• Visible, removable filter selections
• Detailed program information in popups
• Browse matching programs in a sortable list
• Save programs and edit personal notes
• Compare two to four saved programs
• Export saved programs to CSV
