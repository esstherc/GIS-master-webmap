## Reviewed with no row change

The 2025 CSV already contained the current program links for Eastern Michigan University, Penn State MPS Geodesign, Delta State University, and Johns Hopkins University. Eastern Michigan's [GIS page](https://www.emich.edu/geography-geology/programs/geographic-information-science/index.php) returned 403 to the automated reader, but the user opened and confirmed it. A 403 alone is not evidence that a program has ended. These rows remain in the 2026 data.

## Scope and review notes

- Calvin's MPA row is omitted from the 2026 inventory because Calvin's current graduate listing has no MPA. The school has not separately confirmed when that concentration ended.
- Georgia Southern's closure and Elmhurst's 2026–2027 catalog finding are user-confirmed. The other closure and suspension decisions above have current university-list evidence.
- The new Utah Geography MS row uses only verified fields. Duration and detailed coursework are left blank rather than inferred.
- The CCNY degree is an MS in Sustainability in the Urban Environment with a geospatial focus, not a standalone MS in Geospatial Data Sciences. Its delivery mode is left blank because the program page does not specify one.
- Liberty's three MS tracks are stored as three linked records in `data/program_tracks_2026.csv`. They count as one MS degree in the 174-row program inventory.
- The 2026 coordinate file adds campus points for Colorado School of Mines and CCNY. [Mines](https://mapcarta.com/23180864) and [CCNY](https://mapcarta.com/W108239587) coordinates come from OpenStreetMap-derived campus features; the addresses are stated on [Mines](https://www.mines.edu/academics/graduate-academics/gis-and-geoinformatics-msnt/) and [CCNY](https://www.ccny.cuny.edu/oiacer) pages. The Mines program is online; its map point represents the institution's campus.
- This list covers the programs investigated in this audit. A nationwide search for additional schools and a coordinate audit are separate pending work.
